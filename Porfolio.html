It Depends Game Project

IT Depends is a fast-paced, time-based point-and-click puzzle game where you play as an IT worker responsible for keeping critical servers running. You must juggle multiple tasks at once—such as connecting wires, removing malicious data, and solving simple math problems—before their timers run out. Each server has its own countdown, and if any one fails, you lose your job. The challenge comes from managing all three systems simultaneously under pressure, testing your speed, accuracy, and multitasking skills.
